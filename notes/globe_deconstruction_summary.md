# GLOBE DECONSTRUCTION? — Concise Summary

Levi Miller (Purdue industrial engineering, 2014), with co-author/collaborator Alex Kampf (Purdue environmental/ecological engineering, 2014) and Mark Meyer (Ball State geological sciences, 2014), present a ~500-page self-described "geocentrist" (not flat-earther) case against heliocentrism, built around two headline claims they consider decisive, plus a long tail of recycled flat-earth material.

## Core claim #1: Rockets can't work in a true vacuum
Miller's most developed and most-repeated argument: gas-propelled vehicles (rockets, thrusters) require an external medium ("surrounding gases") to push against, so Newton's third law is misapplied to propulsion in a vacuum — thrust from expelled gas alone, without ambient gas to compress against, should be "zero." He states this is a claim "almost all flat earthers" already make; his and Kampf's original contribution is a self-funded ($3,000), dated (November 17, 2023) experiment: a small syringe/toy-car rig on a linear track inside a vacuum chamber, tested with the chamber wall at two different distances, to see whether wall proximity affects how far/fast the "rocket" moves. They name the resulting principle **"Kampf's Law"**: dense-matter (liquid/solid) ejection can generate real reaction thrust, but gas ejection needs an external substrate to compress against — remove the substrate (true vacuum) and thrust goes to zero. This experiment, first teased on p.16 and detailed on pp.79-83, is invoked as "the ultimate smoking gun of this entire book" and is referenced repeatedly (pp.44, 183, 196, 209-210, 232, 258, 265) as grounds for rejecting rocket propulsion, deep-space probe maneuvers, and — by extension — the Moon landings, ISS, and all satellite-based evidence for Earth's motion. They further apply the claim to a real amateur rocket (the 2004 GoFast suborbital launch), speculating its apparent mid-flight deceleration is unexplained by a normal despin mechanism and might indicate an unknown external force.

## Core claim #2: Gravity's control over gases is unproven
Less crisply stated but recurring throughout: Miller repeatedly questions whether spacetime curvature/gravity coherently explains how gases behave — specifically, how early volcanic (toxic) gases could ever have "escaped" Earth's gravitational pull to make room for breathable atmosphere if gravity supposedly holds gas down as a "blanket" or a "container with no lid" (pp.102-104), and whether a collapsing, spinning cloud of gas (the solar-nebula origin story) would actually condense into planets rather than fling apart (p.110). Later, a long ledger of "positive claims" heliocentrists must defend (pp.205-230) frames standard atmospheric physics — hydrostatic equilibrium, the tropopause as a gravitational "lid," gravity preventing gas molecules from flying into space — as unproven assertions rather than settled science. Notably, this is NOT the standard flat-earth "density/buoyancy replaces gravity" argument, which Miller explicitly and repeatedly rejects as "clearly not the case" (p.1) and tells flat earthers to "stop using" (p.197). His position is closer to "gravity's mechanism for confining/sorting gas is asserted, not demonstrated" than to a flat denial that gravity affects gas at all.

## Other major planks (each covered in 1-2 sentences)
- **Bottom-up disappearance / angular resolution**: argues ships, boats, and cities appear to disappear "bottom-up" over water not because of curvature but because of the limits of human/camera angular resolution combined with linear perspective, illustrated with headlight, hallway, and tree-line analogies and long-distance photography (Nikon P900/P1000 zoom shots of a wind farm and Monviso, Italy at 184 miles).
- **Water/hydrostatics**: claims no controlled experiment has ever directly measured water bending to follow Earth's curvature, and proposes a hypothetical 1000-meter "skinny warehouse" test to settle it.
- **Celestial/optics claims**: argues the human eye's spherical geometry (not sky curvature) explains why Polaris appears to rise/fall with latitude; calculates Polaris's angular size as ~18,860× smaller than the eye's resolution limit to argue against its claimed distance; critiques a flat-earth-challenge astrophotographer's Jupiter moon-shadow image as evidence the shadow doesn't align with the Sun.
- **Astronautics/ISS/NASA skepticism**: recycles the standard flat-earth-adjacent "ISS is CGI/wire-work/green-screen" catalogue and Massimo Mazzucco-sourced moon-landing-hoax claims; separately gives an extended, detailed treatment of Bob Knodel's 2018 ring-laser gyroscope experiment from *Behind the Curve*, arguing suppressed elevation-dependent follow-up data actually disproves Earth's rotation (a direct match to a well-documented existing flat-earth argument).
- **Atmospheric co-rotation skepticism**: standard "why doesn't the air's rotational speed vary by altitude, and why is there zero air speed exactly at the pole" argument (a long-standing Rowbotham-lineage claim), presented without attribution.
- **Antarctica / cartography**: unusually, Miller explicitly rejects the standard flat-earth "ice wall, unexplored Antarctica" narrative as "lazy," and instead builds an original, self-labeled-speculative reinterpretation of the 1587 Urbano Monte world map — arguing its apparent "errors" (missing/misshapen landmasses) may reflect real lost geography, that Antarctica's "land of fire" label implies it was once ice-free, and using real magnetic-declination data to sketch a new "Magnetic Declination Removed" flat map with hypothetical unexplored lands ("Lacach," "Patalia," "Galleca," "Vista").
- **Historical/architecture "Tartaria" tangents**: a long final section (roughly pages 240-470) recycling the broader "Tartaria" internet-conspiracy genre — elaborate 19th-century buildings supposedly inconsistent with their claimed construction dates/technology, "mudflood" buried first floors, suspiciously convenient "Great Fires," missing construction records — explicitly credited to other YouTube researchers rather than original to Miller, and explicitly marked by Miller himself as outside the core "shape debate." This culminates in an original "Reset War" theory proposing the 1850-1880 period's ~41 global conflicts (including the American Civil War) were a coordinated pretext for globally rolling out globe-based school curricula, tied to a real historical timeline of national globe-curriculum mandates.

## Rhetorical structure and framing (relevant to how the book argues, not just what)
The book opens with an extended epistemology/cognitive-bias primer (indoctrination, confirmation bias, normalcy bias, dogmatism, Occam's Razor) before any substantive claims, and repeatedly frames the entire project as structurally identical to atheist "deconstruction" of religion — Miller narrates his own break from Christianity in detail as a template, borrows the "falsification is independent of replacement" framing from religious-debate rhetoric, and dedicates the book to Alex Hormozi and atheist debater Matt Dillahunty. It explicitly brands itself "No religions. No beliefs. No biases. No assumptions." while simultaneously drawing on citations from amateur YouTube figures given mock-credentialed titles ("Dr John D. Agnostic, PhD in spectrophotometry"; "Vortexpuppy Agnostic, PhD in mathematics and physics"; "Architecture Researchers, PhD in asking questions") alongside a few real credentialed scientists (Vera Rubin, Svetlana Berdyugina) cited more conventionally. The book's central dialectical device is a "falsification-only" burden-of-proof framework (a "Team A vs Team B" scorecard, pp.205-230) that catalogues 100+ ordinary astronomy facts as unverified "positive claims" heliocentrism must defend, while treating the geocentric side as needing only to demonstrate isolated failures. The historical/architecture material in the back third is explicitly flagged by Miller as speculative and outside the "shape debate" proper.
